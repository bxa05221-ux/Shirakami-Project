# Human Gate Strategic Profile v0.1

## Purpose

The strategic profile makes Human Gate review requirements configurable without
moving decision authority to the AI runtime.

It is intended for situations where strategy depends on the setting itself:
relative positions, asymmetry, assumptions, evidence, alternatives, and the
possibility of returning to an earlier setting.

## Strategic review checks

- Setting — Is the situation definition itself correct?
- Positioning — How are self and counterparty positioned?
- Asymmetry — Knowledge, experience, resources, authority, information, time.
- Assumptions — Which assumptions remain unverified?
- Evidence — What is fact, and what is interpretation?
- Alternatives — Are branches preserved rather than prematurely collapsed?
- Strategy — AI may generate strategies, but cannot select one.
- Reset — Can the review return to the setting when a strategy fails?

## Authority boundary

The profile changes what the human must inspect, not who has authority.

AI may observe, analyze, and generate options. It may not rank options, select a
strategy, or commit a decision.

Human Gate retains the ability to confirm or modify the setting and select the
strategy.

## Example: asymmetric negotiation

A party may be an amateur while the counterparty is a professional. That
asymmetry can be disadvantageous, but it can also create opportunities if the
counterparty makes assumptions about the amateur's knowledge or behavior.

The profile therefore requires asymmetry and underlying assumptions to be
surfaced rather than converted automatically into a weak-side strategy.

## Design rule

> Configure the gate, not the decision.
