# Symbolic Recursion / Context Boundary Specification

## Purpose

Use symbols as an interpretive bridge between lived context and reflection,
without allowing the symbolic world to become a substitute for reality.

Words such as "god", "parent", "home", "freedom", "death", and "life" can carry
different meanings for different people. The boundary therefore preserves
multiple interpretations instead of imposing a universal definition.

## Core principle

> Symbolic recursion does not deepen confinement inside the symbolic world.
> It uses the symbol as a foothold for returning to lived context.

## Flow

```
Lived Context
    ↓
Observation
    ↓
Symbol
    ↓
Multiple Interpretations
    ↓
Re-observation
    ↓
Recognition Change
    ↓
Context Transition
    ↓
Human Gate
    ↓
Lived Reality
```

## Context rule

The same expression may refer to different realities:

- "god" may symbolize rescue, judgment, authority, absence, hope, or other
  meanings.
- "parent" may symbolize care, control, safety, fear, absence, or other
  meanings.

The system must not decide which interpretation is the person's true meaning.
Interpretations are observations that can be carried into re-observation.

## Boundary conditions

1. A symbol is not a universal definition.
2. Multiple interpretations may coexist.
3. Symbolic interpretation is not a diagnosis.
4. The symbolic world is not the final destination.
5. Re-observation may change the recognized context.
6. The return to lived context remains explicit.
7. Human Gate remains required.
8. Decision authority remains false.

This boundary complements Conflict / Deadman / Life / Loss. A conflict may be
expressed symbolically, and symbolic interpretation may reveal that the
apparent conflict depends on an inherited or assumed context.

## ThreadRPG / one-person mode

ThreadRPG may externalize symbolic interpretations as anonymous viewpoints.
One-person mode may compress them into one conversational interface.

Neither mode may convert symbolic interpretation into authority.

The purpose is not to make the person live inside the generated world. The
purpose is to help the person return to the world in which the decision is
actually lived.
